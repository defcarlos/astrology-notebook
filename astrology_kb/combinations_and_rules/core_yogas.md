# Core Yogas: Diagnostic Card

Planetary Yogas connect personal drive (*Purusha*) to specific life outcomes (*Karma Phalas*). Use this card alongside the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md) to decode yoga formations and reconcile opposing results.

---

## 1. Solar Yogas (Flanking the Sun)
Determines how the core ego, authority, and public identity project into the world. Formed by planets in the 2nd and/or 12th houses from the Sun (excluding Moon, Rahu, Ketu):

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Geometry Rule for Models:**
> * The Sun **cannot flank itself**. A planet is 1st from itself, never 12th from itself.
> * **"12th from Sun"** means a **separate planet** occupies the sign immediately preceding the Sun's sign (e.g. if Sun is in Pisces, 12th from Sun is Aquarius).
> * **"2nd from Sun"** means a **separate planet** occupies the sign immediately following the Sun's sign (e.g. if Sun is in Pisces, 2nd from Sun is Aries).
> * **Do not confuse House Rulership with "12th from Sun"**: If the Sun rules the 12th house (Rules: House 12) but occupies the 7th house (Placed In: House 7), it is NOT in the 12th from itself!

| Yoga Name | Formation | Psychological & Material Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vesi Yoga** | Separate planet in 2nd from Sun | Truthful, courageous, good memory, steady material comfort. |
| **Vasi (Vosi) Yoga**| Separate planet in 12th from Sun | Learned, eloquent, charitable, recognized by authorities. |
| **Ubhayachari Yoga**| Separate planets in both 2nd & 12th | Symmetrical strength: high endurance, eloquence, executive capability, great wealth. |
| **Papavesi Yoga** | Malefic (Mars/Saturn) in 2nd from Sun | Sharp/harsh speech, conflict over resources, public disputes, impatient temper. |
| **Subhavasi Yoga** | Benefic (Jupiter/Venus/Merc) in 12th | Pious, ethical conduct, sustained institutional favor, peaceful demeanor. |
| **Misra Ubhayachari**| Benefic on one flank, Malefic on other | High-amplitude trajectory: massive career peaks mixed with continuous opposition and controversy. |
| **Papanapha Yoga** | Malefic in 12th from Sun | Impulsive expenditure, mental restlessness, vitality drainage under stress. |

## 2. Lunar & Angular Yogas (Mind, Status & Legacy)

| Yoga Name | Formation | Diagnostic Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gajakesari Yoga** | Jupiter in Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from Moon | Superior intelligence, lasting institutional fame, ability to overcome adversaries ("lion over elephant"). |
| **Amala Yoga** | Benefic in 10th from Lagna or Moon | Spotless public reputation, lasting legacy, philanthropic instincts, respected professional stature. |
| **Kemadruma Yoga** | No planets in 2nd or 12th from Moon | Emotional isolation, intense forced self-reliance, cyclical mental struggle. |
| **Sunapha / Anapha** | Benefics in 2nd / 12th from Moon | Abundant emotional resources, financial security, and mental calm. |

## 3. Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (The Five Pillars)
Formed when non-luminary planet is in **Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10)** AND in **Own Sign or Exaltation**. Governed by [Planetary Dignity](../core_elements/planets/planetary_dignity.md):

* **Ruchaka (Mars):** Martial vigor, physical courage, engineering prowess, unwavering leadership.
* **Bhadra (Mercury):** Analytical brilliance, commercial genius, razor-sharp communication.
* **Hamsa (Jupiter):** Ethical nobility, spiritual wisdom, revered by leaders.
* **Malavya (Venus):** Aesthetic mastery, magnetism, luxury, refined craftsmanship.
* **Sasa (Saturn):** Structural endurance, mass appeal, commanding authority built over time.

---

## 4. Sankhya & Special Raja Yogas

* **Dama Yoga (Sankhya):** All 7 visible planets occupy exactly 6 signs. Signifies continuous resourcefulness, widespread fame, generosity, and sustained institutional support.
* **Brahma Yoga:** Benefics positioned in angles/trines from kendra lords. Signifies deep scholarship, philosophical purity, high respect from experts, and long-term prosperity.

## 5. Operational Evaluation Rules for Models

1. **Lagna Lord Gatekeeper Rule:** A yoga’s capacity to physically manifest depends on the condition of the **Ascendant Lord (L1)**. If L1 is in Dusthana (6, 8, 12) or in *Mrita Avastha* (see [Planetary Avasthas](../core_elements/planets/planetary_avasthas.md)), the native conceives great ambitions but struggles with execution fatigue.
2. **Polarity Synthesis (Never Cancel):**
   * If a chart contains opposing combinations (e.g. *Vesi* + *Papavesi* or *Amala* + *Papanapha*), synthesize as a **situational stress response**.
   * *Example:* Mars forming both Vesi and Papavesi means the native exhibits visionary courage and public charisma when building, but turns sharp-tongued and abrasive when pressured.
