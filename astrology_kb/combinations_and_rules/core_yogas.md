# Core Yogas: Diagnostic Card

Planetary Yogas connect personal drive (*Purusha*) to specific life outcomes (*Karma Phalas*). Use this card alongside the [Astro Gatekeeper](../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md) and [300 Important Combinations](300_important_combinations.md) to decode yoga formations and reconcile opposing results.

---

## 1. Solar Yogas (Flanking the Sun — *Vesi, Vasi, Ubhayachari*)
Determines how the core ego, authority, and public identity project into the world. Formed by planets (excluding Moon, Rahu, Ketu) in the **2nd** and/or **12th** houses from the Sun:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Geometry Rule for Models:**
> * The Sun **cannot flank itself**. **"2nd from Sun"** is the sign following the Sun's sign; **"12th from Sun"** is the sign preceding the Sun's sign.
> * Do not confuse House Rulership (`Rules: House 12`) with occupying the 12th from the Sun!

| Yoga Name | Formation | Psychological & Material Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vesi Yoga** | Planet(s) in 2nd from Sun | Truthful, balanced vision, good memory, steady material comfort. |
| **Vasi (Vosi) Yoga** | Planet(s) in 12th from Sun | Learned, eloquent, strong memory, charitable, respected by authority figures. |
| **Ubhayachari Yoga** | Planets in both 2nd & 12th from Sun | Symmetrical support around the Sun: executive capability, eloquence, broad shoulders/stamina, and prosperity. |
| **Subhavesi / Subhavasi / Subhobhayachari** | Natural Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) flanking the Sun (2nd, 12th, or both) | Charismatic leadership, diplomatic grace, virtuous reputation, and effortless support from mentors/allies. |
| **Papavesi / Papavasi / Papobhayachari** | Natural Malefics (Mars, Saturn) flanking the Sun (2nd, 12th, or both) | Grit and combative drive, but prone to sharp speech, friction with authorities, or resource pressure under stress. |
| **Misra (Vesi / Vasi / Ubhayachari)** | Both a Benefic and a Malefic flanking the Sun | High-amplitude trajectory: combines diplomatic charm and vision with fierce resilience, navigating both high status and intense opposition. |

## 2. Lunar & Angular Yogas (Flanking & Supporting the Moon)
Formed by planets (excluding Sun, Rahu, Ketu) in the **2nd** and/or **12th** houses from the Moon, or in Kendras from Lagna/Moon:

| Yoga Name | Formation | Diagnostic Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sunapha Yoga** | Planet(s) in 2nd from Moon | Self-made wealth, sharp intelligence, good reputation, and capacity to build tangible assets. |
| **Anapha Yoga** | Planet(s) in 12th from Moon | Magnetic demeanor, emotional dignity, appreciation for comfort/arts, and strong moral character. |
| **Durudhura Yoga** | Planets in both 2nd & 12th from Moon | Emotional and financial cushioning on both sides; enjoys resources, vehicles, and loyal support. |
| **Papanapha / Papasunapha** | Malefics in 12th / 2nd from Moon | Restless mind, impulsive spending (*Papanapha*), or fiercely independent earning style (*Papasunapha*) that requires emotional grounding. |
| **Misra Sunapha / Anapha** | Mixed Benefics + Malefics flanking Moon | Resourceful and adaptable mind that earns through diverse or unconventional channels while managing periodic stress. |
| **Kemadruma Yoga** | No planets in 2nd or 12th from Moon | Emotional self-reliance; feels they must build their own internal safety net without taking support for granted. |
| **Gajakesari Yoga** | Jupiter in Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from Moon | Lasting reputation, emotional generosity, strategic wisdom, and ability to overcome complex obstacles. |
| **Amala Yoga** | Benefic in 10th from Lagna or Moon | Spotless professional ethics, respected public legacy, and charitable leadership. |

## 3. Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (The Five Great-Person Pillars)
Formed when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn sits in a **Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10)** in its **Own Sign or Exaltation**:
* **Ruchaka (Mars):** Martial courage, executive decisiveness, technical/physical mastery, commanding leadership.
* **Bhadra (Mercury):** Analytical brilliance, commercial/architectural acumen, articulate communication.
* **Hamsa (Jupiter):** Ethical nobility, philosophical depth, natural teacher/counselor revered by peers.
* **Malavya (Venus):** Aesthetic mastery, artistic magnetism, diplomatic grace, material refinement.
* **Sasa (Saturn):** Structural endurance, organizational authority, mastery over large systems built through discipline.

## 4. Nabhasa Pattern Yogas & Special Raja Yogas (Engine-Calculated)

* **Veena (Vallaki) Yoga (Sankhya — 7 planets across 7 signs):** Deep appreciation for music, rhythm, design, and the arts; versatile, socially magnetic, and skilled at orchestrating diverse teams.
* **Dama Yoga (Sankhya — 7 planets across 6 signs):** Generous, resourceful, and community-minded; builds wide networks of mutual support.
* **Pasa Yoga (Sankhya — 7 planets across 5 signs):** Pragmatic, talkative, and tightly bound to family/organizational responsibilities; adept at managing complex worldly obligations and earning through persistence.
* **Kedara Yoga (Sankhya — 7 planets across 4 signs):** Grounded "cultivator" archetype; patient, agricultural/real-estate or infrastructure-minded, reliable pillar for family and peers.
* **Kahala Yoga:** Strong mutual reinforcement between intelligence/authority lords (e.g., 4th and 9th lords/Jupiter); grants daring initiative and the capacity to lead large enterprises or teams.
* **Chamara Yoga:** Lagna intimacy with benefic brilliance (or exalted/benefic aspect on Lagna/L1); bestows eloquence, scholarly depth, longevity, and respect from authorities.
* **Neechabhanga Raja Yoga (Cancellation of Debilitation):**
  * *Dispositor Kendra / Exaltation Lord Kendra:* When a `Debilitated` planet's sign lord (dispositor) or exaltation lord sits in a Kendra from the Lagna or Moon.
  * *Diagnostic Meaning:* **The Alchemical Reversal.** The native experiences early-life friction, vulnerability, or imposter syndrome in the houses/karaka domains of the debilitated planet, which forces them to develop extraordinary mastery—ultimately turning that exact area into their greatest source of commanding authority and resilience.

## 5. Operational Evaluation Rules for Models

1. **Lagna Lord Gatekeeper Rule:** A yoga’s capacity to manifest smoothly depends on the **Ascendant Lord (L1)** and the participating planets' [Planetary Avasthas](../core_elements/planets/planetary_avasthas.md).
2. **Polarity Synthesis (Never Cancel):**
   * When opposing yogas coexist in the active list (e.g., *Vasi* + *Papavasi*, or *Anapha* + *Papanapha*), **never cancel them out**.
   * Synthesize them as a **high-capacity Gift + Shadow dynamic**: the positive yoga shows the native's peak constructive expression, while the *Papa/Misra* yoga shows the stress reflex to watch out for (e.g., *Anapha* + *Papanapha* = generous, dignified emotional presence when grounded, paired with a tendency toward impulsive spending or restlessness when fatigued).
