# Combinations and Rules (Yogas)

In Jyotish, a *Yoga* literally translates to a "yoke" or "union." It refers to a specific, predefined combination of planets, signs, or houses that yokes a person to a specific karmic fruit. 

## Key Combination Frameworks

* **[Core Yogas](core_yogas.md)**: The foundational yogas defining major chart architectures (e.g., Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas).
* **[300 Important Combinations](300_important_combinations.md)**: Highly specific classical combinations defining wealth, poverty, health, and status.
