# 300 Important Combinations: Diagnostic Card

Yogas synthesize inherited psychological patterns with material opportunities. Use this card with [Core Yogas](core_yogas.md) and the [Astro Gatekeeper](../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Raja Yogas (Authority, Rank, and Purposeful Leadership)
Formed by the mutual connection (conjunction, mutual aspect, or exchange/Parivartana) of a **Kendra Lord (1, 4, 7, 10 — Action)** and a **Trikona Lord (1, 5, 9 — Grace)**:

* **Yogakaraka Planet:** A single planet that simultaneously rules both a Kendra and a Trikona:
  * **Saturn** for Taurus (rules H9, H10) and Libra (rules H4, H5) Lagnas.
  * **Venus** for Capricorn (rules H5, H10) and Aquarius (rules H4, H9) Lagnas.
  * **Mars** for Cancer (rules H5, H10) and Leo (rules H4, H9) Lagnas.
* **Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga:** Connection between the **9th Lord (Dharma/Father/Fortune)** and **10th Lord (Karma/Career/Authority)**. High indicator of purposeful leadership and professional legacy.
* **Neechabhanga Raja Yoga (Resilience-to-Authority Reversal):**
  * Triggered when a `Debilitated` planet has its sign dispositor or exaltation lord anchored in a Kendra from the Lagna or Moon.
  * Rather than effortless privilege, this produces **earned sovereignty**: early setbacks or sensitivity in that planet's houses compel the native to build unmatched competence and authority over time.
* **Evaluation Rule:** Always cross-check the participating planets' [Planetary Avasthas](../core_elements/planets/planetary_avasthas.md). If a Yogakaraka or Raja Yoga planet sits in `Mṛta (0%)` or `Suṣupti (0%)`, the native still holds the blueprint for leadership (`High Drive / Lacks Resources`), but must consciously build external support systems and pacing to unlock it.

## 2. Dhana Yogas (Wealth Accumulation and Resource Flow)
Formed by combinations linking the wealth-generating houses: **1st (Self), 2nd (Liquid Assets/Kutumba), 5th (Speculative/Creative Intellect), 9th (Fortune), and 11th (Gains/Network Revenue)**:

* **2nd & 11th Lords Linked:** Direct pipeline between earning capacity (H11) and retained capital/assets (H2).
* **5th & 9th Lords Linked:** Wealth generated through intellectual IP, advisory/consulting roles, education, or strategic investments.
* **SAV Confluence:** A Dhana Yoga operating in a house with **> 32 SAV Bindus** in the [Ashtakavarga Matrix](../predictive_and_timing/ashtakavarga_siddhant.md) manifests with high reliability and low friction.

## 3. Viparita Raja Yogas & Dusthana Dynamics (6th, 8th, 12th Houses)
* **Viparita Raja Yoga:** Formed when the lord of a Dusthana (**6th, 8th, or 12th**) is placed in another Dusthana (e.g., L6 in H8/H12, L8 in H6/H12, L12 in H6/H8):
  * *Harsha Yoga (L6 in 6/8/12):* Resilience over competitors, litigation, and daily obstacles.
  * *Sarala Yoga (L8 in 6/8/12):* Fearlessness in crisis, deep research/investigative breakthroughs, longevity protection.
  * *Vimala Yoga (L12 in 6/8/12):* Ethical independence, spiritual contentment, and protection from wasteful drainage.
* **Lagna Lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th:** Personal vitality is drawn toward troubleshooting/service (H6), deep transformation/research (H8), or contemplative/behind-the-scenes mastery (H12). Frame constructively as specialized arenas of mastery (see [The 12 Bhavas](../core_elements/houses_bhavas/the_12_bhavas.md)).
