# 300 Important Combinations: Diagnostic Card

Yogas synthesize inherited psychological patterns with material opportunities. Use this card with [Core Yogas](core_yogas.md) and the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Raja Yogas (Authority, Rank, and Status)
Formed by the mutual connection (conjunction, aspect, mutual reception) of a **Kendra Lord (1, 4, 7, 10 - Action)** and a **Trikona Lord (1, 5, 9 - Grace)**:

* **Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga:** Conjunction or mutual aspect between Lord of 9 (Dharma) and Lord of 10 (Karma). Highest indication of career authority, public leadership, and fulfilling life purpose.
* **1st & 5th / 1st & 9th Lords:** Aligns personal character (L1) with creative intellect (L5) or institutional luck (L9).
* **Evaluation Rule:** If the planets forming a Raja Yoga are in Enemy signs, Debilitated, or in [Mrita Avastha](../core_elements/planets/planetary_avasthas.md), status arrives with heavy compromise, public battles, or sudden reversals.

---

## 2. Dhana Yogas (Wealth Accumulation and Gains)
Formed by combinations linking wealth houses: **1st (Self), 2nd (Assets), 5th (Speculative intellect), 9th (Fortune), and 11th (Revenues/Profits)**:

* **2nd Lord in 11th / 11th Lord in 2nd:** Unbroken flow of capital and liquid revenue.
* **5th & 9th Lords combined:** Effortless generation of wealth through strategic intellect and advisory roles.
* **SAV Confluence:** A Dhana Yoga operating in a sign/house with **>32 Bindus** in the [Ashtakavarga Matrix](../predictive_and_timing/ashtakavarga_siddhant.md) produces explosive material success.

---

## 3. Arishta Yogas (Karmic Friction, Debts, and Depletion)
Formed when lords of **Dusthanas (6th - Conflict/Debt, 8th - Crises/Chronic illness, 12th - Loss/Confinement)** heavily afflict the Ascendant, Sun, or Moon:

* **Viparita Raja Yoga:** Lord of a Dusthana placed in another Dusthana (e.g., L6 in 8th, L8 in 12th, L12 in 6th).
  * *Diagnostic Meaning:* Success achieved specifically through the downfall of rivals, sudden systemic crises, or extreme crisis management. The native thrives in chaos.
* **Lagna Lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th:** Personal vitality is repeatedly drawn into solving crises, dealing with opposition, or undergoing periods of forced solitude. (See [The 12 Bhavas](../core_elements/houses_bhavas/the_12_bhavas.md)).

---

## 4. Operational Rule for LLMs
* **Check Dignity & Friendships:** Verify if the participating planets are allies in the [Planetary Friendships](../core_elements/planets/planetary_friendships.md) matrix. Friendly alliances make yogas manifest smoothly; inimical relationships (`Adhi Shatru`) create intense internal friction during manifestation.
