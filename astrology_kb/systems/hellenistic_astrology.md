# Hellenistic Astrology & Sect: Diagnostic Card

Hellenistic astrology (Mediterranean tradition, 1st c. BCE – 7th c. CE) maps the objective conditions of *Fate* and *Fortune* using Whole Sign Houses (*Topoi*), **Sect** (*Hairesis* — Day vs. Night chart), and Planetary Joys. Reference alongside [Hellenistic Places](../core_elements/houses_bhavas/hellenistic_places.md) and the [Astro Gatekeeper](../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Determining Sect (Day vs. Night Chart) from the Context Table

You can determine whether any natal chart is a **Day Chart (Diurnal)** or **Night Chart (Nocturnal)** directly from the **Sun's `Placed In` house**:

| Sun's `Placed In` House | Chart Sect | Sect Leader | Most Constructive Benefic | Most Constructive Malefic | Out-of-Sect Malefic (Highest Friction) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Houses 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, or 12** | **Day Chart (Diurnal)** *(Sun above horizon)* | **Sun** | **Jupiter** (Operates at peak generosity & wisdom) | **Saturn** (Operates as disciplined structure & patience) | **Mars** (Prone to impulsive heat, conflict, or impatience) |
| **Houses 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6** | **Night Chart (Nocturnal)** *(Sun below horizon)* | **Moon** | **Venus** (Operates at peak relational & artistic grace) | **Mars** (Operates as focused courage & protective stamina) | **Saturn** (Prone to heavy self-doubt, delays, or rigidity) |

## 2. Core Hellenistic Diagnostic Principles

1. **Sect Modification of Malefics (Mars vs. Saturn):**
   * In a **Day Chart (Sun in H7–H12)**, **Saturn** is the *Malefic of Sect* (its discipline is constructive and architectural), whereas **Mars** is *Out of Sect* (its heat is more volatile and requires conscious channeling).
   * In a **Night Chart (Sun in H1–H6)**, **Mars** is the *Malefic of Sect* (its drive is strategic, protective, and resilient), whereas **Saturn** is *Out of Sect* (its restrictions feel heavier and require deliberate warmth and pacing).
2. **Witnessing & Aversion (Visibility to the Ascendant):**
   * **Witnessing Places (1st, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th, 11th):** Connected to the 1st Place by major aspect; life areas the native can directly steer.
   * **Places in Aversion (2nd, 6th, 8th, 12th):** In "blindspots" to the 1st Place; represent unexpected obligations, service/health routines (6th), deep transformations (8th), or private retreat (12th).
3. **Planetary Joys (Peak House Alignment):**
   * **Mercury in 1st** (Helm) | **Moon in 3rd** (Goddess) | **Venus in 5th** (Good Fortune) | **Mars in 6th** (Bad Fortune — constructively defeats obstacles) | **Sun in 9th** (God) | **Jupiter in 11th** (Good Spirit) | **Saturn in 12th** (Bad Spirit — mastery in solitude).
   * Whenever a planet occupies its **Joy House**, highlight this as an natural environmental buff that smooths out its expression.
