# Hellenistic Astrology: Framework Card

Hellenistic astrology (Mediterranean tradition, 1st c. BCE – 7th c. CE) focuses on mapping the objective conditions of *Fate* (Heimarmene) and *Fortune* (Tyche). Reference alongside the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Core Architectural Pillars

* **Whole Sign Houses (Topoi):** Each sign represents an entire house/place starting with the Ascendant sign as the 1st Place (see [Hellenistic Places](../core_elements/houses_bhavas/hellenistic_places.md)).
* **Sect (Diurnal vs. Nocturnal):** Determines whether planets function constructively (benefics of sect) or destructively (malefics out of sect):
  * *Day Chart (Sun above horizon):* Jupiter and Saturn are favored.
  * *Night Chart (Sun below horizon):* Venus and Mars are favored.
* **Witnessing & Aversion:** Places that cannot form a classical aspect to the 1st Place (2nd, 6th, 8th, 12th) represent blindspots and unexpected liabilities.
* **Planetary Joys:** Specific places where planets operate with highest natural efficacy (e.g. Mercury in 1st, Mars in 6th, Jupiter in 11th).
