# Astrological Systems

This directory outlines the fundamental philosophies and structural methodologies of the primary astrological systems used for analysis and synthesis.

## Active Frameworks

* **[Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)](vedic_astrology.md)**: The "Science of Light," focusing on karma, lunar mansions (Nakshatras), and predictive timing (Dashas).
* **[Hellenistic Astrology](hellenistic_astrology.md)**: The study of fate and fortune based on ancient Greek traditions, emphasizing sect, planetary joys, and time-lord techniques like profections.
