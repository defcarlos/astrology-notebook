# Vedic Astrology (Jyotish): Framework Card

Jyotish ("The Science of Light") maps the ripening of personal *Karma* (action and reaction) across lifetimes. Reference alongside the [Astro Gatekeeper](../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Core Architectural Pillars

* **Sidereal Zodiac (Nirayana):** Anchored to fixed stellar constellations using the Ayanamsa offset (see [Mathematical Foundations](../mathematical_and_astronomy/mathematical_astrology.md)).
* **Lunar Mansions (Nakshatras):** 27 divisions of $13^\circ 20'$ that govern the emotional mind, subconscious motivations, and predictive timing cycles (see [Nakshatras Reference](../core_elements/nakshatras/nakshatras_reference.md)).
* **Divisional Charts (Vargas):** Microscopic harmonics of the primary chart. Key vargas:
  * **D-9 (Navamsa):** Structural soul fortitude, inner resilience, and latter-half-of-life maturity.
  * **D-10 (Dashamsha):** Professional archetype, career leadership, and worldly achievements.
* **Vimshottari Dasha Clock:** 120-year hierarchical timing system that sequences when planetary potentials manifest (see [Dasha Systems](../predictive_and_timing/dashas_and_yogini.md)).
* **Ashtakavarga Matrix:** Point-based quantified scoring system measuring structural capacity and transit resistance (see [Ashtakavarga Matrix](../predictive_and_timing/ashtakavarga_siddhant.md)).
