# Transits (Gochar): Diagnostic Card

While [Dasha Systems](dashas_and_yogini.md) reveal what karmic fruit is ripe, Transits (*Gochar*) act as the delivery trigger. Major events require agreement between the active Dasha lords and transiting planets. Use alongside the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Transits from the Natal Moon (Chandra Lagna)

In Vedic astrology, transit efficacy is measured primarily from the natal Moon sign. Auspicious transit houses (`* = Auspicious` in summaries) allow the planet to deliver favorable results:

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL RULE FOR MODELS: Transits from Moon vs. Natal House Placement**
> In chart summaries (e.g. `Transits from Moon: Sun: Libra (H7)`), the `(H#)` notation represents the **house counted from the natal Moon (Chandra Lagna)**, NOT the natal house from the Ascendant:
> * **Example:** If natal Moon is in Aries and transiting Sun is in Libra, the Sun is in the 7th house *from the Moon* $\rightarrow$ labeled `Sun: Libra (H7)`.
> * **Do NOT confuse this with the natal 7th house:** If the native has a Virgo Ascendant, Libra is physically **Natal House 2** (finances, family, speech), NOT House 7 (partnerships)!
> * **Operational Rule:** Use the Moon-relative position `(H#)` to evaluate *mental ease, subjective friction, and whether the transit is auspicious (`*`)*; map the transiting sign back to the *natal Ascendant house* to determine the concrete life domain affected.

## 2. The Double Transit Rule (Jupiter & Saturn)

Major life milestones (promotions, marriages, relocations, breakthroughs) manifest when the heavy outer planets agree:

* **The Trigger:** When transiting **Saturn** and transiting **Jupiter** *both* aspect or occupy the same house (or cast mutual aspects onto that house's lord), that area of life is intensely activated.
* **The Synergy:** Saturn provides real-world structure, commitment, and finality; Jupiter provides grace, resources, and expansion.

## 3. Ashtakavarga Transit Verification
Never judge a transit without checking the natal bindu count in the [Ashtakavarga Matrix](ashtakavarga_siddhant.md):
* **Transit over $\ge$ 28 Bindus:** The transit delivers its highest positive potential smoothly.
* **Transit over $<$ 25 Bindus:** Even an "auspicious" transit faces structural bottlenecks, fatigue, or delays.
* **Saturn/Mars over $<$ 20 Bindus:** Critical vulnerability zone; triggers acute crises or forced liquidation.
