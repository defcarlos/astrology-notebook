# Predictive and Timing Techniques

A static natal chart shows the *promise* of a life, but predictive techniques are required to determine *when* those promises will manifest. 

## Predictive Methodologies

* **[Dashas & Yogini](dashas_and_yogini.md)**: Planetary period systems that act as the macro-managers of time.
* **[Transits (Gochar)](transits_gochar.md)**: The current, real-time positions of the planets acting as micro-triggers.
* **[Saral Ashtakavarga Siddhant](ashtakavarga_siddhant.md)**: A mathematical point system used to precisely evaluate the strength of transits.
