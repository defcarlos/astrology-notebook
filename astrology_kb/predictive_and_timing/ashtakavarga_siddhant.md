# Ashtakavarga (SAV) Matrix: Diagnostic Card

Ashtakavarga provides an objective mathematical score (Bindus / points) for each of the 12 signs and houses, measuring inherent structural capacity and transit permeability. Reference alongside the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Natal House Scoring Scale (SAV Bindus)

The baseline average for any house is **28 points** (Total across 12 houses = 337 points):

| Score Range | Classification | Natal Interpretation | Transit Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **> 32 Points** | **Peak Zone** | Area of effortless abundance, strong institutional support, and durable gains. | Transiting planets (even natural malefics) yield constructive, stabilizing success. |
| **29 – 32 Points** | **Strong Zone** | High capacity; solid returns on personal effort. | Transits operate smoothly with minimal friction. |
| **25 – 28 Points** | **Neutral Baseline** | Standard capacity; outcomes correspond exactly to labor invested. | Standard transit results; depends on planet's dignity. |
| **20 – 24 Points** | **Strained Zone** | Structural bottlenecks; chronic effort required to maintain progress. | Transits trigger delays, fatigue, or operational obstacles. |
| **< 20 Points** | **Depleted Zone** | Energetic drain, vulnerability, recurring loss, or burnout. | Malefic transits (Saturn, Rahu, Mars) cause acute crises or collapse. |

---

## 2. Analyzing SAV Distribution Patterns

When reading an array like `SAV Bindus (Houses 1-12): [25, 29, 35, 29, 18, 30, 25, 30, 32, 29, 36, 17]`:

* **Peak vs. Low Dynamics:**
  * Identify the **Highest House (Peak)** and **Lowest House (Trough)**.
  * *Example (Peak H11 @ 36 vs. Low H12 @ 17):* House 11 (Revenue, Network, Ambition) is overflowing with capacity, enabling massive corporate and social gains. Simultaneously, House 12 (Expenses, Seclusion, Physical Limits) is severely depleted at 17, indicating that heavy professional output directly strains physical health and forces involuntary isolation or medical crises.
* **The 5th House Drain (<20 Bindus):**
  * If House 5 has low bindus (e.g. H5 @ 18), speculative ventures, emotional peace, and subordinate relationships face acute friction.
* **Upachaya Growth (Houses 3, 6, 10, 11):**
  * High bindus in Upachayas guarantee that challenges are steadily overcome and capacity expands over time.

## 3. Operational Rules for Transit Interpretation

1. **Slow-Moving Transit Triggers (Saturn & Jupiter):**
   * Check the SAV score of the house being transited in [Transits (Gochar)](transits_gochar.md).
   * Saturn moving through a house with **>30 bindus** builds lasting empires.
   * Saturn moving through a house with **<22 bindus** demands radical austerity, brings depression, or reveals systemic failure.
2. **Confluence with Dashas:**
   * A challenging Dasha period (see [Dashas & Timing](dashas_and_yogini.md)) is significantly mitigated if the active planets transit through high-SAV houses (>28).
