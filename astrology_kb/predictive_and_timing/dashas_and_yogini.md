# Dasha Timing Systems: Diagnostic Card

Vimshottari Dasha is the primary clock of karmic activation in Vedic astrology, sequencing the ripening of latent natal potentials across a 120-year cycle. Use alongside the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md) and [Transits (Gochar)](transits_gochar.md).

---

## 1. The Dasha Hierarchy (The 3-Tier Clock)

* **1. Mahadasha (MD - Macro Canvas / Years):**
  * Governs the overarching life chapter, psychological tone, and dominant environment (e.g. Rahu MD = ambition, foreign contacts, tech/disruption, sudden expansion).
* **2. Antardasha (AD - Focal Actor / Months):**
  * The specific planet executing events within the Mahadasha canvas (e.g. Saturn AD within Rahu MD = disciplined restructuring, intense workload, endurance tests).
* **3. Pratyantardasha (PD - Immediate Trigger / Weeks):**
  * The minute timing mechanism that delivers specific conversations, psychological moods, or acute physical turning points.

## 2. Geometric Relationship Between MD Lord & AD Lord

To diagnose the ease or friction of any sub-period, calculate the mutual house distance between the **Mahadasha (MD) Lord** and the **Antardasha (AD) Lord**:

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL RULE FOR MODELS: Inclusive Circular Counting (Do NOT Subtract House Numbers)**
> Mutual house distance between two planets must be calculated by **counting inclusive forward around the 12 houses**, NOT by simple arithmetic subtraction ($5 - 3 \neq 2$):
> * **Example (MD Lord Rahu in House 5, AD Lord Saturn in House 3):**
>   * Count forward from **Saturn (H3)** to **Rahu (H5)**: H3 (1), H4 (2), H5 (3) ➔ **3rd house distance**.
>   * Count forward from **Rahu (H5)** to **Saturn (H3)**: H5 (1), H6 (2), H7 (3), H8 (4), H9 (5), H10 (6), H11 (7), H12 (8), H1 (9), H2 (10), H3 (11) ➔ **11th house distance**.
>   * **Result:** This is an auspicious **3/11 relationship (Upachaya Growth)** through sustained effort.
>   * **Anti-Hallucination Warning:** NEVER do $5 - 3 = 2$ to declare a 2/12 (Dwir-Dwadasha) crisis!

## 3. Transition Rules for Upcoming Sub-Periods (AD Shifts)

When analyzing an upcoming Antardasha shift (e.g., from *Rahu-Saturn* to *Rahu-Mercury*):
1. **Compare Dignity & Karaka:** Check the incoming lord's [Chara Karaka](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md) and [Dignity](../core_elements/planets/planetary_dignity.md). If the incoming planet is the **Atmakaraka** (e.g., when the incoming planet is the dynamic soul karaka), the new sub-period will force major soul realignment and personal reinvention.
2. **Examine Natal House Lordships:** The incoming lord activates the affairs of the houses it rules (e.g. an incoming lord ruling L10 and L1 brings public career and self-direction directly to the forefront).
3. **Cross-Check with Transits:** Verify the incoming lord's current transit score in the [Ashtakavarga Matrix](ashtakavarga_siddhant.md). A score >28 guarantees a triumphant transition.
