# Decoding the Zodiac (Rashis)

To properly synthesize a planet's placement, one must deeply understand the Rashi (Sign) it occupies. A Rashi is not just a psychological archetype; it is a complex intersection of elements, modalities, and ruling planets.

## Core Attributes of a Rashi
When evaluating a sign, consider the following structural components:
1.  **Ruler (Lord):** The planet that governs the sign. The condition of this ruling planet in the chart dictates the overall health of the sign.
2.  **Element (Tattva):** Fire (Dharma/Inspiration), Earth (Artha/Material), Air (Kama/Desire), or Water (Moksha/Liberation).
3.  **Modality:** Movable/Cardinal (Initiating), Fixed (Sustaining), or Dual/Mutable (Adapting).
4.  **Dosha:** The Ayurvedic constitution of the sign (Vata, Pitta, Kapha).

## The 12 Rashis (Zodiac Signs)

### 1. Aries (Mesha)
* **Ruler:** Mars
* **Element / Modality:** Fire / Movable (Cardinal)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Pitta / The Ram
* **Archetype:** The pioneer, the warrior, independent, impulsive, highly driven, headstrong, and pioneering. Represents the birth of the ego and physical vitality.

### 2. Taurus (Vrishabha)
* **Ruler:** Venus
* **Element / Modality:** Earth / Fixed
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Vata / The Bull
* **Archetype:** The builder, steadfast, practical, sensual, stubborn, and resource-oriented. Represents accumulated wealth, speech, and material sustenance.

### 3. Gemini (Mithuna)
* **Ruler:** Mercury
* **Element / Modality:** Air / Dual (Mutable)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Tridoshic (Mixed) / The Twins
* **Archetype:** The communicator, intellectual, adaptable, curious, restless, and highly analytical. Represents conscious thought, short travels, and duality.

### 4. Cancer (Kataka)
* **Ruler:** Moon
* **Element / Modality:** Water / Movable (Cardinal)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Kapha / The Crab
* **Archetype:** The mother, emotional, intuitive, protective, nurturing, and sensitive. Represents the internal emotional baseline, the home, and psychological roots.

### 5. Leo (Simha)
* **Ruler:** Sun
* **Element / Modality:** Fire / Fixed
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Pitta / The Lion
* **Archetype:** The king, noble, authoritative, creative, loyal, proud, and performative. Represents individual power, natural intelligence, and dharma.

### 6. Virgo (Kanya)
* **Ruler:** Mercury
* **Element / Modality:** Earth / Dual (Mutable)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Vata / The Virgin
* **Archetype:** The perfectionist, analytical, service-oriented, critical, organized, and pure. Represents daily labor, health/disease management, and problem-solving.

### 7. Libra (Thula)
* **Ruler:** Venus
* **Element / Modality:** Air / Movable (Cardinal)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Vata/Kapha / The Scales
* **Archetype:** The diplomat, harmonious, relationship-focused, fair, aesthetic, and compromising. Represents the marketplace, marriage, and balance with the "other."

### 8. Scorpio (Vrischika)
* **Ruler:** Mars (co-ruled by Ketu)
* **Element / Modality:** Water / Fixed
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Kapha / The Scorpion
* **Archetype:** The alchemist, intense, secretive, transformative, deeply emotional, and investigative. Represents the occult, sudden changes, vulnerabilities, and rebirth.

### 9. Sagittarius (Dhanus)
* **Ruler:** Jupiter
* **Element / Modality:** Fire / Dual (Mutable)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Pitta / The Centaur (Archer)
* **Archetype:** The philosopher, optimistic, belief-driven, expansive, dogmatic, and adventurous. Represents higher knowledge, gurus, law, and long journeys.

### 10. Capricorn (Makara)
* **Ruler:** Saturn
* **Element / Modality:** Earth / Movable (Cardinal)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Vata / The Crocodile (Sea-Goat)
* **Archetype:** The executive, ambitious, disciplined, pragmatic, status-driven, and structural. Represents public action, career, karma, and time's physical limits.

### 11. Aquarius (Kumbha)
* **Ruler:** Saturn (co-ruled by Rahu)
* **Element / Modality:** Air / Fixed
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Vata / The Water Bearer
* **Archetype:** The visionary, humanitarian, eccentric, systematic, detached, and rebellious. Represents large networks, the collective, societal gains, and universal truth.

### 12. Pisces (Meena)
* **Ruler:** Jupiter
* **Element / Modality:** Water / Dual (Mutable)
* **Dosha / Symbol:** Kapha / The Two Fishes
* **Archetype:** The mystic, compassionate, imaginative, boundary-less, escapist, and spiritual. Represents ultimate liberation (moksha), the subconscious, isolation, and surrender.

## Rasi-Graha Interactions (Agent Execution Rule)
When generating reports, an agent must synthesize the planet's core nature with the sign's elemental and modal environment:
* **Exaltation/Debilitation:** Does the sign naturally support the planet? (e.g., The Sun's natural authority aligns perfectly with Aries' cardinal fire, leading to exaltation. The Sun's independent nature struggles in Libra, a sign focused on compromise, leading to debilitation).
* **Sign Lord Dignity:** Always check the condition of the planet that rules the sign. If Mars is in Taurus, the result heavily depends on whether Taurus's ruler (Venus) is well-placed or afflicted.
