# Divisional Charts (Vargas): Diagnostic Card

In Vedic astrology, divisional charts (*Vargas*) provide microscopic cross-sections of the primary birth chart (D-1 Rasi). While D-1 shows the physical tree and environment, the Vargas reveal the internal seed and whether that tree bears lasting fruit. Use alongside the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md) and [Planetary Dignity](planets/planetary_dignity.md).

---

## 1. The Core Vargas in Chart Summaries (`D9 / D10`)

When reviewing the `D9 / D10` column in planetary tables (e.g. `Leo / Sagittarius`):

* **D-9 Navamsa (9th Harmonic — Soul Fortitude, Dharma & Marriage):**
  * **Significations:** Inner psychological resilience, spiritual destiny, marriage/partnership quality, and the trajectory of the **second half of life (post-age 32–35)**.
  * **Diagnostic Function:** Acts as the underlying "root system" for D-1 placements. A planet's true long-term endurance is governed by its sign condition in the Navamsa.
* **D-10 Dashamsha (10th Harmonic — Career & Public Legacy):**
  * **Significations:** Public professional rank, executive authority, vocation, and concrete institutional accomplishments.
  * **Diagnostic Function:** Reveals how career planets (**Sun, Mercury, Saturn, 10th Lord, and Amatyakaraka**) execute power in the professional world.

## 3. The Vargottama Rule (Same Sign in D-1 and D-9)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Vargottama Definition:**
> When any planet (or the Ascendant) occupies the **exact same zodiac sign in both the D-1 (`Sign` column) and D-9 (first sign of the `D9 / D10` column)**, it is **Vargottama** ("best division").
>
> **Diagnostic Manifestation:**
> * Operates with the structural resilience, confidence, and protection of being in its **Own Sign (Swakshetra)**.
> * Grants unyielding internal conviction and stamina in the houses it occupies and rules.

## 4. D-1 versus D-9 Dignity Trajectory Matrix

Compare a planet's D-1 `Dignity` with its D-9 sign dignity (using Section 2 above) to diagnose how that planet matures over time:

| D-1 (Rasi) Dignity | D-9 (Navamsa) Sign Dignity | Real-World Trajectory & Maturity (Post-Age 32) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High** (Exalted / Own) | **High** (Exalted / Own) | **Unshakable Mastery:** Lifelong competence, high internal stamina, and durable materialization of success. |
| **High** (Exalted / Own) | **Low** (Debilitated) | **Fragile Promise:** Strong external visibility or early momentum that requires careful maintenance to avoid burnout in mid-life. |
| **Low** (Debilitated / Enemy) | **High** (Exalted / Own) | **Hidden Strength (Raja Yoga Maturity):** Early imposter syndrome or external obstacles give way to deep internal mastery and success in the second half of life. |
| **Low** (Debilitated) | **Low** (Debilitated) | **Intense Karmic Forge:** Requires conscious pacing, boundaries, and structural support to manage recurring friction. |

## 5. Jaimini *Karakamsha* (The Atmakaraka's D-9 Sign)

In Jaimini astrology, the **D-9 Navamsa sign occupied by the `Atmakaraka (Self/Soul)`** is called the **Karakamsha**:
* **How to Find It:** Locate the planet labeled `Atmakaraka (Self/Soul)` in the `Karaka` column, and read the **first sign** in its `D9 / D10` column (e.g., if Mars is Atmakaraka with `D9 / D10: Aquarius / Scorpio`, the native's *Karakamsha* is **Aquarius**).
* **Diagnostic Meaning:** Reveals the soul’s inner spiritual motivation and ideal vocational element:
  * *Fire Karakamsha (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius):* Driven by autonomous leadership, pioneering vision, and dharmic principle.
  * *Earth Karakamsha (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn):* Driven by tangible craftsmanship, architecture, resource stewardship, and practical mastery.
  * *Air Karakamsha (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius):* Driven by intellectual synthesis, systems thinking, diplomacy, and collective/humanitarian networks.
  * *Water Karakamsha (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces):* Driven by emotional depth, healing, investigative research, and spiritual liberation.

## 6. Operational Guardrail on `D9 / D10` Columns
* In condensed chart summaries (`*_llm_context.md`), the `D9 / D10` column provides **Zodiac Signs**, **NOT** divisional house numbers. Never invent a D-9 or D-10 house number! Evaluate the planet by its **D-9 / D-10 sign archetype and sign dignity**.
