# Divisional Charts (Vargas): Diagnostic Card

In Vedic astrology, divisional charts (*Vargas*) provide microscopic cross-sections of the primary birth chart (D-1 Rasi). While D-1 shows the physical tree, the Vargas reveal whether that tree bears fruit. Use alongside the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md) and [Planetary Dignity](planets/planetary_dignity.md).

---

## 1. The Core Vargas in Chart Summaries (`D9 / D10`)

When reviewing the `D9 / D10` column in planetary tables (e.g. `Leo / Sagittarius`):

* **D-9 Navamsa (9th Harmonic — The Soul's Fortitude):**
  * **Significations:** Inner psychological resilience, spiritual destiny, partnership karma, and the trajectory of the **second half of life (post-age 30–35)**.
  * **Diagnostic Function:** Acts as the reality check for D-1 placements. A planet is only as strong as its dignity in the Navamsa.
* **D-10 Dashamsha (10th Harmonic — Career & Public Legacy):**
  * **Significations:** Public professional rank, executive authority, social status, and institutional accomplishments.
  * **Diagnostic Function:** Indicates how effectively career planets (Sun, Mercury, 10th Lord) manifest power and recognition in the outer world.

---

## 2. The Vargottama Rule (Same Sign in D-1 and D-9)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Vargottama Definition:**
> When a planet occupies the **exact same zodiac sign in both the D-1 (Rasi) and D-9 (Navamsa)** charts, it is termed **Vargottama** ("best division").
>
> **Diagnostic Manifestation:**
> * The planet operates with the durability, confidence, and protection of being in its **Own Sign (Swakshetra)**.
> * It grants the native unyielding core conviction and physical/psychological stamina in the domains governed by that planet.
> * Even if the planet is in a neutral or enemy sign in D-1, being Vargottama elevates it to high functional power.

## 3. D-1 versus D-9 Dignity Shift Matrix

Models must compare a planet's D-1 dignity with its D-9 sign placement to diagnose trajectory over time:

| D-1 (Rasi) Dignity | D-9 (Navamsa) Dignity | Real-World Trajectory & Psychological Manifestation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High** (Exalted / Own) | **High** (Exalted / Own) | **Unshakable Mastery:** Lifelong ease, high internal stamina, and durable materialization of success. |
| **High** (Exalted / Own) | **Low** (Debilitated / Enemy) | **Fading Promise:** High early charisma, talent, or public luck that deteriorates under sustained stress or collapses in the latter half of life. |
| **Low** (Debilitated / Enemy) | **High** (Exalted / Own) | **Late-Blooming Triumph (Neecha Bhanga):** Early struggle, self-doubt, or severe restrictions that transform into profound resilience, expertise, and success after age 30. |
| **Low** (Debilitated / Enemy) | **Low** (Debilitated / Enemy) | **Systemic Resistance:** Chronic vulnerability; requires conscious external discipline and compensatory structures. |

## 4. D-10 Dashamsha Career Rules

> [!NOTE]
> **Operational Guidance for Models on D9 / D10 Columns:**
> In condensed chart summaries (`[subject]_llm_context.md`), the `D9 / D10` column provides the **Zodiac Sign** occupied by each planet in that division (e.g. `Leo / Sagittarius`), **NOT** divisional house numbers.
> * A D-10 Lagna is typically omitted in condensed payloads. Do **NOT** invent a D-10 house number or assume the D-10 sign matches the D-1 natal house!
> * **Evaluate Sign Archetype & Dignity:** Assess the planet's sign comfort in D-10 (e.g., Mercury in Gemini in D-10 operates in its Own Sign with high technical mastery; Sun in Sagittarius operates with expansive visionary authority).
> * **If D-10 House Positions Are Explicitly Provided:**
>   * **Kendra / Trikona in D-10:** Planet possesses strong public visibility, institutional backing, and ability to hold high executive rank.
>   * **Dusthana (6, 8, 12) in D-10:** Career involves continuous troubleshooting, organizational crisis management, independent solo ventures, or working outside conventional corporate hierarchies.
