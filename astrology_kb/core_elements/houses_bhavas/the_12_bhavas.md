# The 12 Bhavas (Houses): Diagnostic Card

The 12 Bhavas map the complete spectrum of human life experience across the four Purusharthas (Dharma, Artha, Kama, Moksha). Use alongside the [Astrology Writer](../../astrology-writer/SKILL.md) and [Hellenistic Places](hellenistic_places.md).

---

## 1. Structural Classifications

* **Kendras (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th - The Pillars):** Action, worldly manifestation, and foundational pillars. Planets here have high public visibility.
* **Trikonas (1st, 5th, 9th - The Trines of Grace):** Inherent talent, past-life merit (*Purva Punya*), ethical luck, and divine protection.
* **Dusthanas (6th, 8th, 12th - The Houses of Crisis):**
  * *6th (Ari):* Acute illness, open enemies, litigation, debts, and daily problem-solving.
  * *8th (Randhra):* Sudden transformations, chronic health, surgeries, occult research, crises, and vulnerability.
  * *12th (Vyaya):* Involuntary expenses, isolation, foreign lands, sleep, hospitals, and spiritual release.
* **Upachayas (3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th - Houses of Growth):** Houses that improve over time through grit, persistence, and continuous effort.
* **Marakas (2nd and 7th - Houses of Transition):** 12th from 3rd (2nd) and 12th from 8th (7th). See Longevity Analysis.

### Kendra vs. Maraka Precedence Rule (Crucial for 7th House)
* **Primary Function (Worldly Life):** The 7th house is first and foremost an auspicious **Kendra (Quadrant of Action)** governing marriage, significant business partnerships, diplomacy, public relations, and trade. Planets here or ruling this house actively support public engagement and relationship building.
* **Secondary / Latent Function (Maraka Status):** The Maraka (death-inflicting / transition) role of the 2nd and 7th houses is strictly **latent and secondary**. It is ONLY triggered during specialized inquiries into [Longevity Analysis](../../specialized_branches/longevity_analysis.md) or terminal health crises during active Maraka Dasha/Antardasha periods.
* **Anti-Hallucination Rule for Models:** Never interpret a planet in the 7th house or the 7th lord as dangerous, fatal, or destructive simply because the 7th house is a Maraka sthana. In standard personality, career, psychological, or relationship consultations, treat the 7th house strictly as an auspicious Kendra.

## 2. Lordship Transfer Formula (L-Numbers)

A house lord transfers the agenda of the house it rules into the house it occupies:

* **L1 (Ascendant Lord) in 6th, 8th, or 12th:** The native’s physical vitality and identity are intimately tied to navigating crises, handling adversaries, or seeking deep solitude.
* **L10 (Career Lord) in 6th:** Career involves continuous troubleshooting, organizational dispute resolution, healing, or intense daily execution against opposition.
* **L8 in 8th (Own House):** Protects longevity and grants intense psychological resilience through crisis, but intensifies chronic vulnerabilities.
* **L9 or L10 in Kendra/Trikona:** Generates [Raja Yoga](../../combinations_and_rules/300_important_combinations.md) and high social status.
