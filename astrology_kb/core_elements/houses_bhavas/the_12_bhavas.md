# The 12 Bhavas (Houses): Diagnostic Card

The 12 Bhavas map the complete spectrum of human life experience, environment, and relationships across the four Purusharthas (*Dharma, Artha, Kama, Moksha*). Use alongside the [Astrology Writer](../../astrology-writer/SKILL.md) and [Hellenistic Places](hellenistic_places.md).

---

## 1. Structural Classifications

* **Kendras (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th — The Pillars of Action):** Worldly manifestation, foundational structure, and active agency. Planets placed here have maximum public and personal impact.
* **Trikonas (1st, 5th, 9th — The Trines of Grace):** Inherent talent, past-life merit (*Purva Punya*), ethical wisdom, providence, and natural flow.
* **Dusthanas (6th, 8th, 12th — The Houses of Friction & Transformation):**
  * *6th (Ari):* Daily labor, service, acute/curable illness, debts, litigation, competitors, and problem-solving.
  * *8th (Randhra):* Longevity, sudden transformations, chronic conditions, surgeries, joint/in-law assets, inheritances, and deep research/occult knowledge.
  * *12th (Vyaya):* Solitude, foreign lands/emigration, sleep/dreams, intimacy (*Shayana Sukha*), expenses, hospitalization, and spiritual liberation (*Moksha*).
* **Upachayas (3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th — Houses of Growth):** Houses that improve with age through grit, skill acquisition, and persistent effort. Natural malefics (Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Sun) excel in Upachaya houses.
* **Marakas (2nd and 7th — Houses of Transition):** 12th from the 3rd (2nd) and 12th from the 8th (7th).

### Kendra vs. Maraka Precedence Rule (Crucial for 7th House)
* **Primary Function (Worldly Life):** The 7th house is first and foremost an auspicious **Kendra (Quadrant of Action)** governing marriage, committed partnerships, diplomacy, contracts, and public trade.
* **Secondary / Latent Function (Maraka Status):** The Maraka role of the 2nd and 7th houses is strictly **latent** and triggered ONLY during specialized inquiries into [Longevity Analysis](../../specialized_branches/longevity_analysis.md) or terminal health crises. Never interpret the 7th house or 7th lord as fatal or destructive in standard personality, career, or relationship readings.

## 2. Lordship Transfer Formula (How House Lords Carry Meaning)

In the context table, every visible planet has a `Placed In` house and one or more `Rules (Lord of)` houses. **A house lord carries the agenda and people of the house(s) it rules into the physical house it occupies:**

* **L1 (Ascendant Lord):** Directs the native's core vitality, attention, and physical life path into the house it occupies (e.g., L1 in H4 focuses life around home, mother, and emotional security; L1 in H6/H8/H12 ties identity to problem-solving, crisis mastery, or behind-the-scenes depth).
* **L2 (Wealth & Family of Origin Lord):** Shows where liquid resources are earned/spent and how early family values express (e.g., L2 in H11 creates a strong wealth flow through networks; L2 in H6 earns through service, medicine, law, or technical troubleshooting).
* **L3 (Younger Siblings & Courage Lord):** Channels personal initiative, communication, and sibling dynamics into its occupied house.
* **L4 (Mother & Home Lord):** Shows the condition of the mother, domestic peace, and property (e.g., L4 in H6 links the mother or domestic environment with hard work, service, or health duties; L4 in H10 brings domestic/property themes into public career).
* **L5 (Children & Creative Intellect Lord):** Channels creative authorship, romance, and progeny into its occupied house.
* **L7 (Spouse & Partnership Lord):** Shows the arena where the partner is met and how marriage/partnerships operate (e.g., L7 in H6 suggests a partner in service/healthcare or a relationship built around shared daily work).
* **L9 (Father, Dharma & Fortune Lord):** Channels paternal influence, mentorship, and higher grace into its occupied house (e.g., L9 in H7 links the father and ethical fortune directly to public partnerships and visibility).
* **L10 (Career & Public Authority Lord):** Shows the primary vehicle for professional reputation (and secondary paternal authority). L9 + L10 connection forms **Dharma-Karmadhipati Raja Yoga**.
* **L11 (Elder Siblings & Gains Lord):** Shows the source of major financial realizations, social alliances, and elder sibling dynamics.
* **Dusthana Lords (L6, L8, L12):** Bring friction, debt/service (L6), sudden transformation (L8), or release/foreign themes (L12) into the house they occupy—unless placed in another Dusthana (*Viparita Raja Yoga*, turning crisis into triumph).

## 4. Derived Houses (*Bhavat Bhavam*) & Special Relational Rules

### A. The Dual-House Rule for the Father (9th House + 10th House)
When asked about the **Father**, always synthesize **both the 9th House and the 10th House** alongside the **Sun** and **Bhratrukaraka**:
* **9th House & 9th Lord:** Reveals the father’s *inner character, dharmic guidance, philosophy, and protective blessings* toward the native.
* **10th House & 10th Lord (7th from the 4th House of the Mother):** Because the 4th house is the Mother, the 7th from the 4th (the 10th house) represents her partner—the Father! It reveals the father's *external career stature, authority style, public expectations, and role-modeling in the world*.

### B. Other Key Derived Houses (*Bhavat Bhavam*)
Counting $N$ houses inclusive from a starting house reveals secondary relational domains:
* **Siblings (Both 3rd & 11th Houses):** Always check **House 3** (younger siblings, peer courage) AND **House 11** (elder siblings, supportive peer networks) alongside **Mars**, **Jupiter**, and the **Bhratrukaraka (BK)**.
* **In-Laws & Marital Finances (8th House = 2nd from 7th):** The 8th house is the 2nd house (wealth and family) counted from the 7th house (spouse).
* **Maternal Relatives (6th House = 3rd from 4th):** The 6th house is the 3rd house (siblings) counted from the 4th house (mother).
* **Grandchildren (9th House = 5th from 5th):** The 9th house is the 5th house (children) counted from the 5th house.
* **Career Execution & Public Trade (7th House = 10th from 10th):** The 7th house reinforces the 10th house of career, showing how one's profession interfaces with clients and the public.

### C. *Karako Bhava Nashaya* (Significator in Its Own Signifying House)
When a living relative's natural significator (*Naisargika Karaka*) sits physically inside the house it signifies—specifically **Sun in 9th** (Father), **Moon in 4th** (Mother), **Mars in 3rd** (Younger Siblings), **Jupiter in 5th** (Children), or **Venus in 7th** (Spouse)—do **not** interpret it fatalistically. Instead, diagnose it as **heightened sensitivity or perfectionism** around that relationship: the planet shines cleanly on the abstract qualities of the house, but the interpersonal bond with that living relative requires conscious space and realistic expectations unless cushioned by `Own Sign`/`Exalted` dignity or Jupiter's aspect.
