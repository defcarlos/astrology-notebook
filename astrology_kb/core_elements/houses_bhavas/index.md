# The Houses (Bhavas / Places)

While planets are the agents and signs are the environments, the Houses are the specific fields of experience where the action takes place (e.g., career, marriage, health, wealth).

* **[The 12 Bhavas (Vedic)](the_12_bhavas.md)**: The sequential breakdown of the 12 houses, their significations, and their categorical strengths (Kendras, Trikonas, Dusthanas, Upachayas).
