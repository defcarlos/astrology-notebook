# Hellenistic Places (Topoi): Diagnostic Card

In Hellenistic astrology, houses are called *Places* (Topoi) utilizing Whole Sign Houses. Places measure the vantage point of the native's life and whether planets can "witness" the Ascendant. Use alongside [The 12 Bhavas](the_12_bhavas.md) and the [Astrology Writer](../../astrology-writer/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Witnessing vs. Aversion (Visibility to Native)

* **Supportive Places (Witnessing the 1st):**
  * The 1st, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th, and 11th places configure to the Ascendant via Ptolemaic aspect (sextile, square, trine, opposition). Matters here are directly visible and manageable by the native.
* **Places in Aversion (The Blindspots):**
  * **2nd, 6th, 8th, and 12th Places:** Cannot make a classical aspect to the 1st Place.
  * They represent domains the native cannot directly see or control: unexpected expenses (2nd/12th), illness/daily conflict (6th), and sudden crises/mortality (8th).
