# Planetary Aspects: Diagnostic Card

An aspect (*Drishti* = "sight" or desire) defines how a planet projects its energy from its occupied house into other areas of life. Use alongside the [Astro Gatekeeper](../../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md) and [The 12 Bhavas](../houses_bhavas/the_12_bhavas.md).

---

## 1. Vedic Parashari Drishti (Sign-to-Sign Reach)

In Vedic astrology, aspects apply to the **entire target sign/house**, regardless of degree.

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL RULE FOR MODELS: Relative Distance vs. Absolute House Number**
> Aspect numbers represent **relative distance counted from the planet's physical location**, NOT the absolute house numbers of the natal chart:
> * **Inclusive Counting Rule:** Always count the planet's occupied house as **House 1**, the next house forward as **2**, and so on.
> * **Concrete Saturn Example (Saturn physically in House 3):**
>   * **3rd house from itself** (H3=1, H4=2, H5=3) ➔ Aspects **House 5** (NOT House 3!).
>   * **7th house from itself** (H3=1 ... H9=7) ➔ Aspects **House 9** (NOT House 7!).
>   * **10th house from itself** (H3=1, H4=2, H5=3, H6=4, H7=5, H8=6, H9=7, H10=8, H11=9, H12=10) ➔ Aspects **House 12** (**NEVER House 10!**).
> * **Negative Constraint:** Saturn in House 3 does **NOT** aspect House 10. A planet in House X only aspects House 10 if it is physically placed in House 1.

### Parashari Special Aspect Rules (Counted Forward from Planet):
* **Universal 7th Aspect:** All planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) cast a full aspect on the **7th house from their location** (directly opposite / 180°).
* **Mars (The Striker):** Aspects the **4th, 7th, and 8th houses away from itself** (protective boundary and crisis/transformation).
* **Jupiter (The Benefactor):** Aspects the **5th, 7th, and 9th houses away from itself** (protective trines of wisdom and dharma). Elevates and protects any house it touches.
* **Saturn (The Taskmaster):** Aspects the **3rd, 7th, and 10th houses away from itself** (structural pressure, persistent labor, karmic duty).
* **Rahu & Ketu:** Aspect the **5th, 7th, and 9th houses away from their placements**.

### Decoding Payload / Context Strings
When reading summary context lines such as:
* `Saturn aspects Venus (3rd House)` ➔ Means: *Saturn casts its 3rd special aspect onto Venus* (because Venus is 3 houses away from Saturn). It does **NOT** mean Venus is in House 3, nor that Saturn aspects natal House 3.
* `Mars aspects Jupiter, Saturn (4th House)` ➔ Means: *Mars casts its 4th special aspect onto Jupiter* (Jupiter is 4 houses away from Mars).

## 2. Tajika Aspects (Degree-Based Orbs)
Tajika aspects introduce precise optical geometry. Unlike sign-based aspects, their strength is measured strictly by **degree orb**:

| Aspect Type | Angle | Orb Tightness | Psychological & Manifestation Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conjunction** | 0° | < 1.0° (Acute) | Total fusion of planetary drives; operating as a single unit. |
| **Sextile** | 60° | < 1.0° (Acute) | Fluid, constructive cooperation between complementary elements (e.g. Earth & Water). |
| **Square** | 90° | < 1.0° (Acute) | High tension, forced adaptation, external clashes demanding structural change. |
| **Trine** | 120° | < 1.0° (Acute) | Effortless harmony, shared elemental flow, mutual enhancement of talents. |
| **Opposition**| 180°| < 1.0° (Acute) | Intense polarization, direct confrontation, or need for conscious relational integration. |

### Orb Intensity Scale:
* **Orb ≤ 1.0° (Exact / Acute):** Immediate, high-intensity life events and unyielding psychological focus. (e.g., *Saturn sextile Venus at 0.0° orb* = absolute precision and structural mastery over aesthetic design).
* **Orb 1.1° – 3.0°:** Active psychological influence; noticeable in personality and timing.
* **Orb > 3.0°:** Diffused background influence.

## 3. Core Synthesis Rules for Models

1. **Own House Protection Rule:** A planet aspecting its **Own House** (Swakshetra) acts as a powerful shield, elevating and protecting that house regardless of whether the planet is a natural malefic or benefic.
2. **Benefic vs Malefic Aspect Impact:**
   * A house aspected by Jupiter or Venus is cushioned against severe crises.
   * A house aspected by Mars or Saturn without benefic counter-aspects undergoes repeated pressure and stress.
3. **Cross-Reference:** Check [Planetary Friendships](planetary_friendships.md) to see if aspecting planets are mutual friends or enemies.
