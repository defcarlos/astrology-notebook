# Planetary Avasthas: Diagnostic Card

While [Planetary Dignity](planetary_dignity.md) defines environmental comfort, an **Avastha** dictates a planet's internal vitality, alertness, and physical capacity to manifest karma. Use alongside the [Astrology Writer](../../astrology-writer/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Baladi Avasthas (Physical Age & Manifestation Capacity)
Calculated strictly from the exact longitudinal degree of the planet within a 30° sign:

| Degrees in Odd Signs (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sag, Aqu) | Degrees in Even Signs (Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Cap, Pis, Sco) | Avastha (State) | Kinetic Manifestation Capacity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0° – 6°** | **24° – 30°** | **Bala (Infant)** | **25%** (Immature; needs guidance and time) |
| **6° – 12°** | **18° – 24°** | **Kumara (Youth)** | **50%** (Energetic and ambitious, but lacks endurance) |
| **12° – 18°** | **12° – 18°** | **Yuva (Adult)** | **100% (Prime)** (Full manifestation power; prime vitality) |
| **18° – 24°** | **6° – 12°** | **Vriddha (Old)** | **10%** (Rich in wisdom, but low physical follow-through) |
| **24° – 30°** | **0° – 6°** | **Mrita (Dead)** | **0% – 5%** (Physically depleted; unable to execute agenda alone) |

---

## 2. Jagradadi Avasthas (State of Consciousness)
Determines how awake or responsive a planet is to its duties:
* **Jagrat (Awake):** Planet in Exaltation or Own Sign. Fully conscious; delivers clear, definitive, unmistakable results.
* **Swapna (Dreaming):** Planet in Friend's or Neutral Sign. Partially conscious; results are inconsistent, delayed, or require external catalysts.
* **Sushupti (Sleeping):** Planet in Enemy Sign or Debilitated. Unconscious; karma manifests blindly, passively, or through painful lessons.

---

## 3. Deeptadi Avasthas (Psychological Mood)
Primary mood indicator during planetary periods ([Dashas](../../predictive_and_timing/dashas_and_yogini.md)):
* **Deepta (Radiant):** Exalted. Imparts visionary courage, abundance, and authority.
* **Swastha (Self-Contained):** Own Sign. Brings stability, comfort, and steady progress.
* **Mudita (Delighted):** Great Friend's Sign. Brings cooperative joy, social harmony, and popularity.
* **Shanta (Peaceful):** Friend's Sign. Balanced, calm, and methodical.
* **Deena (Anxious/Humble):** Neutral Sign. Mediocrity, doubt, or excessive caution.
* **Dukkhita (Distressed):** Enemy Sign. Mental anguish, conflict, and recurring hurdles.
* **Khala (Malicious):** Debilitated. Deprivation, questionable ethics, or feeling cornered.
* **Kopi (Agitated):** Combust or defeated in planetary war. Restless volatility and sudden frustration.

## 4. The Critical Avastha Override Rule

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Dignity does not override Mrita Avastha.**
> If a planet is in its Own Sign or Exalted (Jagrat/Deepta) but sits in the **Mrita (Dead)** degree range (e.g. Mars in Aries at 29.1°), the model must diagnose this as:
> **"High Drive / Lacks Resources"**: The native possesses genuine genius, fierce internal ambition, and high vision (Awake/Deepta), but their physical body, organizational resources, or external environment will repeatedly fail to provide the kinetic stamina needed to execute without severe exhaustion.
