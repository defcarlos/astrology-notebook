# The 7 Visible Planets: Diagnostic Card

In Jyotish, planets are called *Grahas* ("seizers of consciousness"). They act as cosmic focal points that channel specific psychological functions and life domains. Use alongside the [Astro Gatekeeper](../../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md) and [The 12 Bhavas](../houses_bhavas/the_12_bhavas.md).

---

## 1. Quick Reference Matrix

| Planet (Graha) | Natural Quality | Element | Primary Karaka Roles | Psychological Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sun (Surya)** | Mild Malefic (Heat) | Fire | Soul (Atma), Father, Government, Vitality | Ego baseline, self-respect, public authority, purpose |
| **Moon (Chandra)** | Benefic (Waxing) / Malefic (Waning) | Water | Mind (Manas), Mother, Public, Emotional Baseline | Emotional security, intuition, adaptability, public rapport |
| **Mars (Mangala)** | Malefic (Aggressive) | Fire | Energy, Courage, Real Estate, Siblings, Tech/Surgery | Drive, execution, defensive boundaries, combative will |
| **Mercury (Budha)** | Neutral (Takes on associations) | Earth | Intellect, Speech, Commerce, Logic, Communication | Analytical reasoning, trading, networking, humor |
| **Jupiter (Guru)** | Supreme Benefic | Ether/Fire | Wisdom, Wealth, Children, Gurus, Higher Law | Expansion, providence, optimism, ethical discernment |
| **Venus (Shukra)** | Benefic | Water | Marriage, Arts, Luxury, Vehicles, Diplomacy | Relational harmony, aesthetics, sensual fulfillment, design |
| **Saturn (Shani)** | Supreme Malefic | Air | Longevity, Hard Labor, Structure, Delays, Karma | Endurance, discipline, reality testing, detachment |

## 2. Diagnostic Rules for Models

1. **Benefic vs. Malefic Natural Tendency:**
   * Natural Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, well-associated Mercury, waxing Moon) protect, soothe, and expand whatever house they occupy or aspect.
   * Natural Malefics (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, Sun) bring friction, demand effort, or cut away attachments in their occupied/aspected houses.
2. **Functional Nature (Lordship Overrides):**
   * A natural malefic that rules auspicious houses (e.g. Saturn ruling L5 and L6 for Virgo Lagna) becomes a *functional instrument of duty and effort*.
   * Always check [Planetary Dignity](planetary_dignity.md) and [Planetary Avasthas](planetary_avasthas.md) to determine if the planet has the physical resources to deliver its promise.
3. **Cross-References:** Check [Planetary Aspects](planetary_aspects.md) for reach, and [Planetary Friendships](planetary_friendships.md) for internal cooperation.
