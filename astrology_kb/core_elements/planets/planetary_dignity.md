# Planetary Dignity & Strength: Diagnostic Card

A planet's capacity to deliver results depends on its **Dignity** (environmental comfort) and its **Shadbala** (raw kinetic strength). Use alongside the [Astro Gatekeeper](../../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md) and [Planetary Avasthas](planetary_avasthas.md).

---

## 1. Hierarchy of Positional Dignity (State of Comfort)

1. **Exaltation (Ucha):** 100% capacity. Perfectly supported by the sign; delivers peak results easily.
2. **Moolatrikona (Root Trine):** 75%–80% capacity. The planet's active "office"; operates with purposeful efficiency.
3. **Own Sign (Swakshetra):** 50%–60% capacity. "At home"; protected, comfortable, and self-reliant.
4. **Great Friend's Sign (Adhi Mitra):** Highly supported by host ruler via [Planetary Friendships](planetary_friendships.md).
5. **Friend's Sign (Mitra):** Welcome, functional cooperation.
6. **Neutral Sign (Sama):** Neither helped nor harmed; outcome depends heavily on aspects and house.
7. **Enemy's Sign (Shatru):** Friction, resistance, and delayed manifestations.
8. **Great Enemy's Sign (Adhi Shatru):** Actively attacked or deprived of resources by host ruler.
9. **Debilitation (Neecha):** Lowest state. Distorted, starved of resources; requires intense conscious effort to redirect.

Shadbala calculates the actual kinetic energy of a planet to force its agenda into reality:

| Planet | Minimum Required Score | Strong Status | Weak Status |

| **Sun (Surya)** | **390** points | $\ge 390$ (High vitality/authority) | $< 390$ (Low confidence/vitality) |

| **Mercury (Budha)** | **420** points | $\ge 420$ (Razor intellect/logic) | $< 420$ (Nervous strain/indecision) |
| **Jupiter (Guru)** | **390** points | $\ge 390$ (Broad vision/morals) | $< 390$ (Over-optimism/poor advice) |

*(Note: Rahu and Ketu do not have Shadbala calculations).*

## 3. The State-Versus-Energy Diagnostic Matrix

| Dignity (State) | Shadbala (Energy) | Diagnostic Persona | Real-World Manifestation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High** (Exalted/Own) | **High** ($\ge$ Threshold) | **Sovereign Master** | Flawless, easy manifestation of success and resources. |

## 4. The Dispositor Rule
Never judge a planet solely by its sign. Check the **Dispositor** (the ruler of the sign the planet occupies). If the dispositor is exalted or strong, the resident planet is rescued and stabilized. If the dispositor is afflicted, the resident planet suffers regardless of its own dignity.

## 5. Upstream Modifiers: Retrograde `(R)` and Combustion `(C)`

When planets in the summary table carry upstream modifier flags, apply these operational rules:

### A. Combustion `(C)` (Conjunction with the Sun)
* **Diagnostic Meaning:** The planet is astronomically conjunct the Sun and "scorched" by solar heat.
* **Manifestation:**
  * **External / Physical:** The physical significations of the planet (and the houses it rules) are obscured, fatigued, or struggle for independent public recognition.
  * **Internal / Spiritual:** The planet's intellectual and creative drives become intensely fused with the ego/Sun, creating extreme personal conviction, sharp focus, or spiritual insight.

### B. Retrograde `(R)` (Vakra — Motional Inversion)
* **Diagnostic Meaning:** The planet is closest to Earth, granting maximum *Chestha Bala* (kinetic persistence).
* **Dignity Alteration Rules:**
  * **Exalted + Retrograde `(R)`:** Inherent brilliance, but prone to stubbornness, erratic execution, over-promising, or sudden reversals. Does not deliver smooth exalted results.
  * **Debilitated + Retrograde `(R)`:** Gains intense compensatory drive. The native refuses to accept limitations, turning early weakness into breakthrough mastery (*Neecha Bhanga* effect).
  * **Neutral / Friend + Retrograde `(R)`:** Unconventional, non-dogmatic problem solving; re-evaluates rules and works backwards from solutions.

### C. Combined `(R, C)` (Both Retrograde & Combust)
* Deeply internalized, unconventional, and stubborn genius. Operates entirely on internal conviction rather than external consensus.
