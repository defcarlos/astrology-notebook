# Rahu & Ketu: Diagnostic Card

Rahu and Ketu are *Chhaya Grahas* (shadow points)—astronomical intersection points of the Sun and Moon's orbits where eclipses occur. They represent the primary evolutionary axis of the chart. Use alongside the [Astro Gatekeeper](../../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md) and [Planetary Dignity](planetary_dignity.md).

---

## 1. Core Polarities

| Attribute | Rahu (North Node / Head of Dragon) | Ketu (South Node / Tail of Dragon) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Karmic Vector** | **Evolutionary Hunger:** Areas of inexperience, intense drive, obsession, and radical future ambition. | **Past-Life Mastery:** Areas of innate talent, apathy, spiritual detachment, and sudden release. |
| **Archetype** | The innovator, rebel, foreigner, illusionist, technology disrupter. | The monk, ascetic, surgeon, mystic, disintegrator. |
| **Material Expression** | Rapid, erratic expansion, breaking taboos, high worldly ambitions. | Sudden contraction, loss, deep research, rejection of material vanity. |
| **Psychological Mood** | Obsession, anxiety, imposter syndrome, insatiable thirst. | Disconnection, hyper-criticism, spiritual clarity, acute introversion. |

---

## 2. Diagnostic Evaluation Rules for Models

1. **The Dispositor Chameleon Rule:**
   * Nodes possess no signs of their own. They **absorb and violently magnify** the nature of the planet ruling the sign they occupy (the Dispositor), as well as any planet closely aspecting them.
   * *Example:* Rahu in Capricorn in House 5 absorbs Saturn’s structural drive, directing it into relentless, unconventional technological and creative innovation.
2. **Nodal Avasthas (Avastha Override):**
   * If Rahu or Ketu sits in *Mrita Avastha* (e.g. Rahu at 3.4° in an even sign = Mrita), the intense hunger is felt internally, but the native frequently experiences systemic delays or fatigue in translating that hunger into material satisfaction.
3. **Aspects of the Nodes (Relative Trinal Reach):**
   * Both Rahu and Ketu cast full trinal aspects onto the **5th and 9th houses away from their physical locations** (inclusive count forward through the zodiac), NOT natal House 5 or House 9 of the chart.
   * *Example:* Rahu physically in **House 5** aspects **House 9** (5th from itself) and **House 1** (9th from itself). It does **NOT** aspect House 5.
   * Rahu spreads compulsive expansion, hunger, and disruption to the houses it aspects; Ketu spreads spiritual detachment, doubt, and critique.
