# Planets (Grahas)

In Jyotish, planets are referred to as *Grahas*, meaning "that which seizes" or "that which grasps." They are the active agents of karma.

* **[Rahu and Ketu](rahu_and_ketu.md)**: The shadow planets (Lunar Nodes) and their karmic implications.
* **[Planetary Dignity and Strength](planetary_dignity.md)**: The hierarchical framework (Exaltation, Moolatrikona, Debilitation) and Shadbala rules for evaluating a planet's capacity to act.
* **[Planetary Avasthas (States)](planetary_avasthas.md)**: The specific conditions (Age, Alertness, Mood) of a planet that modify how it delivers its results.
* **[Planetary Aspects (Drishti)](planetary_aspects.md)**: The geometric rules for how planets cast their influence upon other houses and planets.
* **[Planetary Friendships](planetary_friendships.md)**: The matrix of natural and temporary relationships that determine how planets cooperate or conflict.
