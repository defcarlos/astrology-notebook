## 🧠 Detailed Psychological & Karmic Significations (The 27 Mansions)

When evaluating a planet's placement in the JSON payload, refer to the following profiles for individual Nakshatras:

### 1. Ashwini
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Ashwini Kumaras / Horse's Head / Ketu
*   **Psychology:** Represents beginnings, rapid action, and healing. Initiating, energetic, catalytic, and highly independent. Dislikes delays and operates on instinct.
*   **Karma:** Strong execution drive; pioneering action without following established rules.

### 2. Bharani
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Yama (God of Death) / Yoni (Womb) / Venus
*   **Psychology:** Driven by extremes, struggle, and transformation. Represents birth, death, and severe purification. Intensely passionate, artistic, and maternal.
*   **Karma:** Placements undergo dramatic cycles of struggle followed by massive renewal.

### 3. Krittika
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Agni (Fire) / Razor or Flame / Sun
*   **Psychology:** Sharp, critical, honest, and direct. Driven to cut away impurities and establish truth. Protective, courageous, but prone to anger.
*   **Karma:** Sharp, surgical actions; purifying situations by confronting and burning away falsehoods.

### 4. Rohini
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Brahma / Chariot or Temple / Moon
*   **Psychology:** Magnetic, sensual, artistic, and highly nurturing. Represents abundance, growth, and the cultivation of resources. Prone to jealousy and materialism.
*   **Karma:** Attracts support and luxury; experiences high material growth and creative output.

### 5. Mrigashira
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Soma (Moon Elixir) / Deer's Head / Mars
*   **Psychology:** The searcher. Curious, restless, analytical, and highly investigative. Enjoys research, travel, and debating. Seekers of truth.
*   **Karma:** Constant intellectual or physical journeying; resolving problems through detailed search and analysis.

### 6. Ardra
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Rudra / Teardrop or Diamond / Rahu
*   **Psychology:** Stormy, intense, intellectual, and transformative. Driven to dismantle structure, resulting in emotional crises that lead to ultimate clarity.
*   **Karma:** Experiences chaotic events; cleanses the path by using intellect to break down illusions.

### 7. Punarvasu
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Aditi (Cosmic Mother) / Bow and Quiver / Jupiter
*   **Psychology:** Optimistic, restorative, and nurturing. Represents the "return of the light," safety, and second chances. Generous, spiritual, and protective.
*   **Karma:** Highly resilient; possesses the grace and talent to bounce back from failures with wisdom.

### 8. Pushya
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Brihaspati / Flower or Cow's Udder / Saturn
*   **Psychology:** Nourishing, ethical, structured, and highly devoted. Represents spiritual teaching, law, and duty. Generous and deeply caring.
*   **Karma:** Highly operational and structured; thrives by dedicating themselves to service, law, or mentoring.

### 9. Ashlesha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Sarpas (Serpents) / Coiled Serpent / Mercury
*   **Psychology:** Intuitive, hypnotic, protective, and highly strategic. Keen perception, capable of deep psychological insight, but prone to suspicion and manipulation.
*   **Karma:** Navigates complex psychological undercurrents; develops defensive or protective boundaries.

### 10. Magha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Pitris (Ancestors) / Royal Throne / Ketu
*   **Psychology:** Noble, traditional, proud, and authoritative. Seeks honor, respects lineage and history, and expects high respect. 
*   **Karma:** Connects with legacy, structural power, and ancestral patterns; gains leadership positions.

### 11. Purva Phalguni
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Bhaga (Fortune) / Hammock or Couch / Venus
*   **Psychology:** Joyous, relaxed, creative, and luxury-oriented. Focused on rest, relationships, romance, and artistic creation.
*   **Karma:** Material comfort and artistic talent; learns to balance relaxation with responsibility.

### 12. Uttara Phalguni
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Aryaman / Bed or Legs of Cot / Sun
*   **Psychology:** Generous, friendly, relationship-oriented, and reliable. Values contracts, partnerships, and societal contribution. Courteous and helpful.
*   **Karma:** Gains status through alliances; thrives in public partnerships and cooperative projects.

### 13. Hasta
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Savitur (Sun God) / Clenched Fist / Moon
*   **Psychology:** Crafty, dexterous, practical, and highly humorous. Talented with hands (writing, healing, business, magic) and possesses high mental agility.
*   **Karma:** Practical manipulation of matter; achieves success through communication, crafts, or trading.

### 14. Chitra
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Vishwakarma (Cosmic Architect) / Pearl or Gem / Mars
*   **Psychology:** Artistic, structural, designer-oriented, and highly aesthetic. Focuses on form, illusion, beauty, and building new structures.
*   **Karma:** High aesthetic design capability; creates beautiful structures or environments to mask underlying flaws.

### 15. Swati
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Vayu (Wind) / Shoot of Plant / Rahu
*   **Psychology:** Flexible, independent, communicative, and commerce-oriented. Excellent at networking, marketing, and adapting to trends. Restless.
*   **Karma:** Commercial expansion and global movement; networks extensively to build material opportunities.

### 16. Vishakha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Indra & Agni / Archway / Jupiter
*   **Psychology:** Determined, ambitious, competitive, and highly targeted. Driven to achieve status or goals, sometimes creating a divided focus or obsession.
*   **Karma:** Goal-driven efforts; achieves success later in life through endurance and competition.

### 17. Anuradha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Mitra (Friendship) / Row of Lotus Buds / Saturn
*   **Psychology:** Cooperative, loyal, friendly, and occult-oriented. Thrives in networks, values friendship, and explores deeper secrets of life. Resilient.
*   **Karma:** Builds long-term cooperative alliances; grows through persistence and deep research.

### 18. Jyeshtha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Indra / Umbrella or Amulet / Mercury
*   **Psychology:** Analytical, protective, competitive, and power-focused. Seeks to protect the vulnerable, claims leadership, but suffers from hidden fears or pride.
*   **Karma:** Attains high status and mental control; must manage internal fears and pride.

### 19. Mula
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Nirriti / Tied Bunch of Roots / Ketu
*   **Psychology:** Radical, investigative, and deeply transformative. Driven to get to the "root" of everything, even if it requires complete destruction of the surface.
*   **Karma:** Cleansing through loss; strips away superficial structures to find core spiritual truth.

### 20. Purva Ashadha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Apas (Water Goddess) / Winnowing Basket / Venus
*   **Psychology:** Proud, charismatic, confrontational, and highly artistic. Convinced of their victory, passionate, and headstrong. 
*   **Karma:** Worldly desire and battle; wins early victories but must learn compromise.

### 21. Uttara Ashadha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Vishwadevas (Universal Gods) / Elephant's Tusk / Sun
*   **Psychology:** Principled, structured, respected, and highly devoted to universal law. Seeks enduring, virtuous victory and works well in alliances.
*   **Karma:** Enduring success through ethical, collective action and persistent labor.

### 22. Shravana
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Vishnu / Three Footprints or Ear / Moon
*   **Psychology:** Receptive, intellectual, and focused on oral knowledge/listening. Excellent student, respects hierarchy, and speaks with impact.
*   **Karma:** Preserves knowledge; grows through study, listening, and organizational structure.

### 23. Dhanishta
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Eight Vasus / Drum or Flute / Mars
*   **Psychology:** Materially successful, timing-oriented, influential, and social. Excellent business sense, musical timing, and network management.
*   **Karma:** Accumulates wealth and resource control; structures operations in perfect harmony with market timing.

### 24. Shatabhisha
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Varuna / Empty Circle / Rahu
*   **Psychology:** Secretive, reclusive, highly analytical, and technological. Keeps plans hidden, manages complex networks, and seeks to heal systemic problems.
*   **Karma:** Strategic work behind the scenes; builds innovative technological or healing networks.

### 25. Purva Bhadra
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Aja Ekapada / Swords or Front of Bed / Jupiter
*   **Psychology:** Intense, idealistic, rebellious, and highly passionate. Capable of extreme dedication, holding radical beliefs, and fighting for ideals.
*   **Karma:** Spiritual or political radicalism; endures high sacrifice to manifest visionary ideals.

### 26. Uttara Bhadra
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Ahirbudhnya / Twins or Back of Bed / Saturn
*   **Psychology:** Wise, patient, calm, and protective. Deep connection to the unconscious, highly imaginative, and capable of enduring restriction with peace.
*   **Karma:** Spiritual stability; acts as a protector, guiding others through deep emotional waters.

### 27. Revati
*   **Deity/Symbol/Planet:** Pushan / Fish or Drum / Mercury
*   **Psychology:** Compassionate, artistic, imaginative, and focused on completion. Guides travelers, cares for detail, and possesses transcendental instincts.
*   **Karma:** Guides transitions and completes cycles; achieves wealth and spiritual liberation (moksha) through surrender of ego.
