# The 27 Nakshatras

The zodiac of 360 degrees is divided into 27 equal parts of 13°20' each. These divisions are the Nakshatras. Because the Moon travels roughly 13°20' per day, it spends about one day in each Nakshatra. 

## Psychological Profiling
The Nakshatra occupied by the Moon at birth (the *Janma Nakshatra*) is the most critical indicator of a person's emotional baseline, mental wiring, and instinctual reactions. 

Each Nakshatra has several layers of meaning:
1.  **Ruling Planet:** Each Nakshatra is ruled by one of the 9 Grahas.
2.  **Ruling Deity:** The Vedic god or goddess associated with the star, providing the core myth and archetype (e.g., Ashwini is ruled by the Ashwini Kumaras, the twin physicians of the gods, indicating a talent for healing).
3.  **Symbol:** A visual metaphor for the star's energy (e.g., a bed, a bow, a tear drop).
4.  **Padas (Quarters):** Each 13°20' Nakshatra is further subdivided into four *Padas* of 3°20'. These padas map directly to the Navamsa (D-9) divisional chart, linking the Nakshatra to the soul's deeper spiritual potential.

## The Engine of Timing
The Nakshatras are not just psychological; they are the mathematical foundation of Vedic predictive timing. The specific degree of the Moon in its birth Nakshatra determines the starting point and balance of the [Vimshottari Dasha system](../../predictive_and_timing/dashas_and_yogini.md).
