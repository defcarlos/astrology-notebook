# Nakshatra Architecture, Padas & Ganas: Diagnostic Card

The 360° sidereal zodiac is divided into **27 Nakshatras** (Lunar Mansions) of $13^\circ 20'$ each, and each Nakshatra is subdivided into **4 Padas** (`P1, P2, P3, P4`) of $3^\circ 20'$ each ($27 \times 4 = 108$ total Padas). Use alongside the [27 Nakshatras Master Reference](nakshatras_reference.md) and [Astro Gatekeeper](../../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Decoding the `(P1 – P4)` Pada Notation in Context Tables

In `*_llm_context.md`, every planet's Nakshatra includes its Pada (e.g., `Pushya (P4)` or `Purva Ashadha (P1)`). Each Pada maps to one of the four elemental motivations (*Purusharthas*) and directly determines the planet's **D-9 Navamsa sign**:

| Pada | Elemental Drive (Purushartha) | Navamsa (D-9) Element | Psychological & Behavioral Expression |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pada 1 (P1)** | **Dharma** (Fire — Aries, Leo, Sagittarius D9) | **Fire** | Pioneering, principled, direct, and self-initiating expression of the Nakshatra. |
| **Pada 2 (P2)** | **Artha** (Earth — Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn D9) | **Earth** | Pragmatic, materializing, structured, and resource-focused expression of the Nakshatra. |
| **Pada 3 (P3)** | **Kama** (Air — Gemini, Libra, Aquarius D9) | **Air** | Communicative, relational, intellectual, and networking-focused expression of the Nakshatra. |
| **Pada 4 (P4)** | **Moksha** (Water — Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces D9) | **Water** | Emotional, intuitive, healing, investigative, and spiritually transformative expression of the Nakshatra. |

## 2. The Three Nakshatra Ganas (Core Temperaments)

The 27 Nakshatras are grouped into three **Ganas** (temperamental tribes), particularly important when evaluating the **Ascendant's Nakshatra** and the **Moon's Janma Nakshatra**:

1. **Deva Gana (Divine / Harmonizing Temperament):**
   * *Nakshatras:* **Ashwini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Pushya, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati**
   * *Expression:* Diplomatic, ethical, peace-seeking, knowledge-oriented, and naturally inclined toward refinement, counseling, and grace.
2. **Manushya Gana (Human / Pragmatic Temperament):**
   * *Nakshatras:* **Bharani, Rohini, Ardra, Purva Phalguni, Uttara Phalguni, Purva Ashadha, Uttara Ashadha, Purva Bhadrapada, Uttara Bhadrapada**
   * *Expression:* Driven by tangible worldly building, ambition, social structures, endurance, and bridging material reality with higher ideals.
3. **Rakshasa Gana (Fierce / Sovereign Temperament):**
   * *Nakshatras:* **Krittika, Ashlesha, Magha, Chitra, Vishakha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha**
   * *Expression:* Fiercely independent, perceptive, boundary-breaking, investigative, and protective. Refuses superficial consensus; excels in crisis mastery, research, and autonomous leadership. *(Always frame Rakshasa Gana constructively as "Fierce/Sovereign Intuition & Independence," never as negative or demonic).*

## 3. Janma Nakshatra & The Engine of Vimshottari Timing

* **The Moon's Nakshatra (`Janma Nakshatra`):** Acts as the primary emotional lens through which the native experiences the world.
* **Dasha Seed Lord:** The planetary ruler of the Moon's Nakshatra sets the opening **Mahadasha** at birth, while the remaining degrees in that Nakshatra determine when the first Dasha transition occurs (see [Dashas & Timing](../../predictive_and_timing/dashas_and_yogini.md)).
