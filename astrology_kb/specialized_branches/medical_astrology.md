# Medical Astrology: Diagnostic Card

Medical astrology synthesizes Jyotish with Ayurveda to diagnose constitutional vulnerabilities and timing of physical ailments. Use alongside [The 12 Bhavas](../core_elements/houses_bhavas/the_12_bhavas.md) and the [Astrology Writer](../astrology-writer/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Ayurvedic Doshas & Planetary Rulers

| Dosha | Elemental Humors | Governing Planets | Vulnerable Organs & Physiological Tendencies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vata** | Air & Ether | **Saturn, Rahu** | Nervous system, spine/bones, colon, dry tissues, anxiety, degenerative disorders. |
| **Pitta** | Fire & Water | **Sun, Mars, Ketu** | Digestion, liver, pancreas, blood, gallbladder, fevers, inflammation, ulcers, blood pressure. |
| **Kapha** | Water & Earth | **Moon, Venus, Jupiter** | Respiratory tract, lungs, mucus, lymphatic system, obesity, fluid retention, diabetes. |
| **Tridosha**| Mixed | **Mercury** | Takes on the dosha of planets aspecting or conjunct it; nervous-gut connection. |

* **Mercury:** Rules the skin.
* **Jupiter:** Rules the fat tissue.
* **Venus:** Rules the semen and the ovum.

## 4. Health Diagnostic Houses

* **1st House (Lagna) & Sun:** Represents constitutional vitality, baseline immune resistance, and the physical brain. Affliction here indicates constitutional vulnerability.
* **2nd and 7th Houses (Maraka Bhavas):** The maraka houses are the 2nd and the 7th. The rulers of houses 2 and 7 are reviewed to indicate when acute vitality crises or destructive influences are active. *(Precedence Rule: Maraka status is strictly latent and analyzed ONLY during terminal health or longevity inquiries; the 7th house remains an auspicious Kendra in standard chart synthesis).*
* **6th House (Ari Bhava):** Acute, curable diseases, inflammation, digestive imbalances, and daily routine strains.
* **8th House (Randhra Bhava):** Chronic, long-term, incurable diseases, surgeries, reproductive/pancreatic organs, and sudden physical traumas.
* **12th House (Vyaya Bhava):** Hospitalization, bed-rest, mental exhaustion, and immune collapse.

## 5. Manifestation Timing Rule

Physical ailments manifest primarily during:
1. The **Mahadasha or Antardasha** of lords of the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses (see [Dasha Systems](../predictive_and_timing/dashas_and_yogini.md)).
2. Heavy transits of Saturn, Rahu, or Mars over natal health points located in houses with **low SAV Bindus (<20)** (see [Ashtakavarga Matrix](../predictive_and_timing/ashtakavarga_siddhant.md)).

**Retrograde Modifiers:** In medical astrology a retrograde planet ruling houses which relate to one's health are found to be damaging in regard to vitality and recuperative abilities.
