# Specialized Branches of Jyotish

While the core principles of Jyotish cover general life themes, certain domains require highly specific rulesets and unique chart configurations (such as specific divisional charts) to analyze accurately. 

## Active Sub-Disciplines

* **[Medical Astrology (Medical Jyotish)](medical_astrology.md)**: The synthesis of astrological placements with Ayurvedic doshas to diagnose and predict physical illness.
* **[Longevity Analysis (Ayurdaya)](longevity_analysis.md)**: The complex mathematical and structural methods used to determine the span of life and timing of physical death.
