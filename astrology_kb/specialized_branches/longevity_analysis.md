# Longevity Analysis (Ayurdaya): Diagnostic Card

Ayurdaya evaluates physical endurance and vital life span. Always cross-reference with [The 12 Bhavas](../core_elements/houses_bhavas/the_12_bhavas.md) and the [Astro Gatekeeper](../astro-gatekeeper/SKILL.md).

---

## 1. Lifespan Categories (Ayur Tiers)

Lifespan is determined by synthesizing the condition of the **Ascendant Lord (L1)**, the **8th House Lord (L8)**, and the **Moon**:

| Category | Lifespan Span | Primary Astrological Indicators |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alpayu (Short Life)** | 0 – 32 Years | L1 and Moon severely afflicted in Dusthanas (6, 8, 12); malefics occupying kendras without benefic rescue. |
| **Madhyayu (Medium Life)** | 32 – 64 Years | Mixed configuration: L1 strong but 8th house challenged, or benefic aspect on an afflicted Moon. |
| **Purnayu (Long Life)** | 64 – 100+ Years | L1 strong and exalted/own sign; Jupiter or Venus in Kendras; 8th house lord protected and dignified. |

---

## 2. Maraka Grahas (Death-Inflicting Planets)

* **The Maraka Houses (2nd and 7th Houses):**
  * *Reason:* The 8th house is longevity. The 12th from any house represents its dissolution. The 12th from the 8th is the **7th House**. The 12th from the 3rd (secondary house of longevity) is the **2nd House**.
* **Maraka Execution Rule:** Physical transitions, terminal crises, or acute life closures occur during the **Dasha or Antardasha of the 2nd or 7th house lords**, or planets occupying those houses, especially when transiting through low-bindu SAV zones (<20).
