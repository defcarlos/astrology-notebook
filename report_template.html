Sudarshana Chakra (Concentric Tri-Wheel)

{{ sudarshana_chakra_svg | safe }}

Holistic Tri-Wheel Synthesis

The **Sudarshana Chakra** is a classical Vedic concentric tri-wheel chart overlaying three key perspectives of the psyche. The 1st house of all three charts is aligned at the top (12 o'clock position) to scan matching sectors instantly:

  • Outer Ring: Udaya Lagna (Ascendant) – The physical body, environment, and concrete circumstances of earthly life.
  • Middle Ring: Chandra Lagna (Moon) – The mental, emotional, and psychological experiences of the individual.
  • Inner Ring: Surya Lagna (Sun) – The soul's spiritual journey, core vitality, and divine alignment.

How to Interpret

Compare the conditions of specific houses (1 through 12) across the rings:

  • Tri-Ring Alignment: When a house is occupied by benefics or receives strong aspects in all three rings, that area of life manifests with extreme ease and harmony.
  • Divergent Paths: When a house is strong in one ring but weak/afflicted in another, it indicates tension. For example, a strong house in Udaya Lagna (physical success) with an afflicted placement in Chandra Lagna (emotional stress) signifies material gain accompanied by inner anxiety.

Kota Chakra (Vulnerability Fort)

The **Kota Chakra** represents a personal fortress calculated from the Janma Nakshatra. It evaluates physical health, psychological protection, and vulnerability to external challenges:

  • Stambha (Pillar): The central heart of the fort. Extremely sensitive; transit of malefics here causes severe disruption.
  • Madhya (Inner Space): Inner courtyards. Represents mental energy and internal resources.
  • Prakara (Wall): The defensive perimeter. Shows boundary control and resilience.
  • Bahya (Exterior): Outposts outside the fort. Indicates interactions with the wider world.

Sarvatobhadra Chakra (Cosmic Radar)

The **Sarvatobhadra Chakra** is an 81-square grid coordinate matrix representing the full celestial sphere. The 28 Nakshatras (including Abhijit) form the outer border:

  • East (Top Row): Krittika to Ashlesha.
  • South (Right Column): Magha to Vishakha.
  • West (Bottom Row): Anuradha to Shravana.
  • North (Left Column): Dhanishta to Bharani.
  • Vedhas (Obstructions): When transiting planets cross coordinates with natal planets horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, they trigger specific events.
{{ kurma_chakra_svg | safe }}

Kurma Chakra (Tortoise Map)

The **Kurma Chakra** maps the 28 Nakshatras onto the body parts of a tortoise representing cardinal geographic directions.

  • Head (East) / Tail (West) / Sides (North/South).
  • Limbs (SE, SW, NW, NE).
  • Application: Used classically in mundane astrology to locate the direction of earthquakes, wars, weather patterns, or career movements relative to the native's birthplace.
